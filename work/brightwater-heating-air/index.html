Questions we hear

Emergencies

Yes. The emergency line rings the technician on call, not an answering service. After-hours visits carry a $99 fee on top of the repair, which Comfort Plan members never pay.

Prices

We charge by the job from a set price book, not by the hour. The $89 service call covers the diagnosis, and it is waived if you go ahead with the repair. You see the price in writing first.

Replace?

A rough rule: multiply the age of the unit by the repair price. Over about $5,000, replacing usually makes more sense. We will give you both numbers and let you choose.

Tune-ups

Once a year for each system: the furnace in the fall and the air conditioner in the spring. A heat pump does both jobs, so it gets two visits.

Brands

We repair every major make of furnace, boiler, air conditioner and heat pump. For new systems we will recommend equipment that fits your house and your budget, and explain why.

Before we come

Clear a path to the furnace and the outdoor unit, put pets in another room, and note when the problem started and any noises or smells. That is all.
