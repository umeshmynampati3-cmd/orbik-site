Do you take my insurance?

Most likely. We are in network with Delta Dental, Cigna, MetLife, Aetna and Guardian, and we file claims for most other PPO plans as an out-of-network office. Call with your card and we will check in a few minutes.

I have a toothache. Can you see me today?

Usually, yes. We hold same-day slots every weekday and on Saturdays. Call before 11am for the best chance. The visit is $95 and we quote any treatment before we start.

When should my child first see a dentist?

By their first birthday, or when the first tooth comes in. Visits under age 3 are free, and we book brothers and sisters back to back so it is one trip for you.

I'm nervous. How do you handle that?

Slowly. You can come in first just to meet us. We tell you what each step will feel like, agree a stop signal, and offer laughing gas for $65 a visit.

Who will I see?

Dr. Nora Halvorsen or Dr. Sam Okafor for exams and treatment, and one of our three hygienists for cleanings. Most families see the same hygienist every time.

What if I need to cancel?

Call or text a day ahead and there is no charge. We send a reminder text two days before every visit.
