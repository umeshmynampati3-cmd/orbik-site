Services and prices

Prices include a consultation, wash and finish. If your hair is very long or very thick, we will tell you before we start, never after.

Cuts

  • Long cut$75

    Shape, layers or a blunt line, finished with a blow-dry. 60 minutes.

Curly hair

    Shape-up between full cuts, with a styling lesson. 45 minutes.

Four chairs and no rush

The studio

A bright corner room with four chairs and two wash basins. We book every appointment with ten spare minutes on the end, so nobody is hurried out of the chair.

The stylists

Maya does colour and hand-painted highlights. Jordan does curly and textured hair. Priya does cuts and wedding styling. Tell us who you want, or we will match you.

Good to know

Booking

One to two weeks for cuts. For colour, especially highlights, two to three weeks. Saturdays fill first.

Cancelling

Please tell us 24 hours ahead. Later than that, or not showing up, costs half the service price.

Curls

With your hair dry, unbrushed, and styled the way you normally wear it, so we can see your curl pattern.

Colour

For big changes, yes. It is free, takes 15 minutes, and includes a patch test 48 hours before your colour.

Paying

Card, Apple Pay or cash. Tips can go on the card or straight to your stylist.
